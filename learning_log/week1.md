What I noted down during class: 
- The basic structure of an Arduino sketch: 'setup()' runs once,
  'loop()' runs forever
- 'pinMode()' sets a pin as input or output
- 'digitalWrite()' turns a pin on (HIGH) or off (LOW)
- 'delay()' pauses the program for a given number of milliseconds
-  "//" instead of "#" for additional notes to self 
- Led = "Light Emitting Diode" 
- pin: longer leg is +, shorter is -
—> never connect + and - to prevent breaking of the board due to short circuit
(-> When + and - are switched, the pin is acting as the ground) 
- Always close breakets and type ";" 

What is the min. value we need, in order to see the blink with our eyes? 
—> 13: shimmering, > 15: blink
- for example: 4000 is 5s 

- in general: place components across the board or vertically, not horizontally!
